Privacy Policy: QuickBooks Online Reporting Connector

This application retrieves accounting records (such as invoices, customers, vendors, bills, payments, accounts, items, and journal entries) from In516ht’s QuickBooks Online company, using the authorization In516ht grants.

The data is stored in In516ht’s own Snowflake account for internal reporting. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for any other purpose. Access to the data is limited to authorized personnel. Authorization tokens are stored securely and used only to retrieve data for In516ht.
